ℹ️ How to Use

  1. Select your preferred language and voice
  2. Click "Start Speaking" and grant microphone permission
  3. Speak clearly into your microphone
  4. The app will transcribe and speak back your words
  5. Adjust speed and pitch to customize the voice output

Note: This app uses the Web Speech API, which works best in Chrome, Edge, and Safari browsers.
